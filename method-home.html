The Method

Intentional care is not a tagline. It is the curriculum.

Anyone can hand you a strip of wax and a checklist. We do the harder, quieter work: teaching you why the skin behaves the way it does, so your hands know what to do before you are ever told.

That is the whole idea behind METHOD. Education that respects your intelligence, care that respects the person in the chair, and a standard we refuse to rush.

Genuine care

For students and guests alike. Every course and every treatment starts from the same place: a real regard for the person in front of us.

The why, not just the how

We teach the science underneath the technique. Understand the skin, the wax, and the reaction, and confidence follows on its own.

Two trainers, every course

Different styles, the same fundamentals. You learn from two sets of hands, which means you leave with range, not a single way of doing things.

Honest and fairly priced

No upsells, no pressure, no theatrics. Clear pricing and straight answers, because trust is the only thing worth building a name on.

The online-to-studio model

How the Academy works.

A deliberate path from your first login to your place in the METHOD community. Foundations online, refinement in person, nothing skipped.

01

Create your profile

Enroll in minutes and set up your learning space. Your coursework, progress, and certificates live in one calm, organized place you return to at your own pace.

02

Learn the foundations online

Work through the science and theory unhurried, on your schedule. Skin biology, product knowledge, safety, and sanitation come first, so technique lands on solid ground.

03

Refine your hands in the studio

Come into the Naples studio for hands-on training with two trainers at your side. This is where knowledge becomes muscle memory, corrected in real time by people who care that you get it right.

04

Get certified

Complete your assessment and earn your METHOD certificate of completion, on a CE-track built to support your continuing education as a licensed professional.

05

Join the Method community

Graduation is a beginning. Step into an ongoing network of METHOD-trained professionals for mentorship, refreshers, and the kind of support that keeps a career growing.
