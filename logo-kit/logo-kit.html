Rules of thumb

  1. Give it room. Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the M in the wordmark.
  2. Never stretch it. Scale proportionally, always.
  3. Do not recolour it outside the colourways above.
  4. Never put the black logo on a dark background. Use the white version.
  5. Do not rebuild the tagline in another font. Use the supplied files.
  6. Below about 40px, drop to the M. The arch hairlines vanish at small sizes.
