# METHOD — Logo Kit

Luxury Waxing & Professional Training · Naples, FL

**Start with `METHOD-Logo-Guide.pdf`.** It is the printable guide: the logo family, clear
space, minimum sizes, colour, what not to do, and an index of every file. This text file is the
short version of the same thing. `logo-kit.html` opens the whole set in your browser.

Everything here was built from the logo you approved. Vectors (SVG, PDF, EPS) can be
scaled to any size, from a business card to a window decal, with no loss of quality.
Rasters (PNG, JPG) are fixed-size and best for web and social.

## Which file do I use?

| I need to... | Use this |
|---|---|
| Put the logo on the website | `01-primary-logo` or `02-horizontal-logo`, SVG |
| Give a file to a printer, sign maker, or embroiderer | `06-print-vector`, the EPS or PDF |
| Post on Instagram or Facebook | `01-primary-logo`, the PNG on cream |

| Add a browser tab icon to the website | `07-website-and-social/favicon.ico` |
| Stamp a photo, sign, or towel | `05-icon-and-submark` |

## The marks

**01 · Primary logo.** The full lockup: arch, M, METHOD., and the tagline. Use this
wherever there is room and it is the first time someone meets the brand.

**02 · Horizontal logo.** The same brand in a wide shape. Built for website headers,
email signatures, and anywhere short and wide.

**03 · Stacked, no arch.** The M, wordmark, and tagline without the arch, for narrow
or busy placements where the arch would get lost.

**04 · Wordmark.** METHOD. on its own, and METHOD. with the tagline. Use when the
arch is already present nearby, or for very small placements.

**05 · Icon and submark.**
- **Icon (the M).** Just the letter. This is your smallest, hardest-working mark:
  favicons, app icons, wax-strip stamps, a foil emboss on a card.
- **Submark (M in the arch).** A near-square version of the arch with the M inside.
  This is the profile-picture mark.

**06 · Print vector.** PDF and EPS of every mark. This is what you hand to a printer,
sign company, or embroidery shop. Send the EPS if they ask for "vector."

**07 · Website and social.** Favicon, Apple touch icon, app icons, square profile
avatars in cream and emerald, and a 1200x630 link-preview image.

**08 · Source.** The original file you supplied, untouched.

## Colourways

- **Black** — the default, on cream, white, or any pale background.
- **White** — reversed, for emerald, black, or a dark photo.
- **Emerald** `#1D392F` and **Copper** `#A56B41` — brand accents, for when a single
  colour is called for and black feels heavy. Available on the primary, horizontal,
  submark, and icon.

Background cream is `#FAF5EB`.

---

## Rules of thumb

1. **Give it room.** Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the
   letter M in the wordmark. Nothing crowds it.
2. **Never stretch it.** Scale proportionally, always.
3. **Do not recolour it** outside the four colourways above.
4. **Do not put the black logo on a dark background.** Use the white version.
5. **Do not rebuild the tagline** in a different font. Use the supplied files.
6. **Below about 40px, drop to the icon.** The hairlines in the arch disappear at
   small sizes. That is what the M-only icon is for.

## One honest note

These files were rebuilt by tracing the artwork you supplied, which was a flat image
rather than an editable design file. The result is true vector and scales cleanly to
any size. If you can get the original editable file from wherever the logo was made,
send it over and it can be rebuilt from the live fonts, which is marginally sharper
at billboard scale. For everything you will actually do, these files are ready to go.
