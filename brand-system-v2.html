Clear space

Keep breathing room equal to the height of the arch opening on every side. Nothing crowds the mark.

Minimum size

Do not place the primary mark below 24px tall on screen, or 0.3in in print. Below that, use the monogram.

Logo don'ts

  • ×

    Don't stretch or condense. Scale the mark proportionally, never distort its width or height.

  • ×

    Don't recolor outside the palette. The mark lives in emerald, copper, ink, or reversed. Nothing else.

  • ×

    Don't add effects. No drop shadows, gradients, outlines, or glow. The mark stays flat and clean.
