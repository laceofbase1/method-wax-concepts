Your First Visit

What to know before you come in.

How long should my hair be?

About the length of a grain of rice, roughly a quarter of an inch. Too short and the wax has nothing to grip. If you have been shaving, give it two to three weeks.

What should I do before my appointment?

Exfoliate gently a day or two before, skip lotion and oil the morning of, and come in clean and dry. Avoid sun, tanning, and strong actives like retinol for a couple of days.

What about afterward?

Keep the area clean and cool for twenty four hours. Skip the gym, hot tubs, pools, and sun. Start exfoliating gently again after a few days to keep ingrowns away.

How often should I come back?

Every four to six weeks keeps you on the growth cycle, which means less hair, finer regrowth, and a more comfortable wax every time.

Do I need to cancel a certain way?

Just call us. There is no cancellation fee. We only ask that you let us know so we can offer the time to someone else.

What if I am on Accutane or using retinol?

Tell us before you book. Accutane means waiting six months after your last dose, and retinol means pausing for five to seven days on the area being waxed.

Do you wax men?

Yes, for most of the menu. Full Face, Full Butt, Butt Strip, and bikini services are offered to women only.
